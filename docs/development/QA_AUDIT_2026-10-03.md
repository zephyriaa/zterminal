## Screenshot clarification: right-border resizing

The user's follow-up screenshot identifies dragging the position's entire right border into the large empty future area. Earlier tests exercised its corner endpoint, which was insufficient coverage of that gesture.

**D15 — High, repaired:** a border drag was interpreted as moving the whole position. Reproduced with a real browser regression before this repair: Long Position's entry changed from timestamp `1791054000000` / price `84765.84962617602` to timestamp `1791056700000` / price `84754.0581183194`. The expected entry was unchanged.

The interaction layer now recognizes the right vertical border and performs a time-only resize. It updates the second anchor's time while retaining entry time/price, target price, stop price, and style. Selection renders a handle at the right edge's entry level, with a horizontal resize cursor. Corner target/stop adjustments, moving from the interior, locking and gesture cancellation retain their existing behavior.

Follow-up verification: `npm test` 237 passed; drawing unit suite 14 passed; typecheck and focused ESLint passed. Independent review found no further actionable issues. Real browser checks for both Long Position and Short Position right-edge resizing passed, including an intentional vertical pointer drift to verify that prices stay fixed. The first updated browser run subsequently timed out during reload; the warmed rerun passed all **26 drawing browser checks**, including persistence and the narrow flyout. The optimized build reported above predates this focused follow-up; it has not been rerun for D15.
